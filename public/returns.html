Returns & Refunds

Contact Woodberry Sports as soon as possible if an item arrives damaged, incorrect or materially different from the confirmed order.

Return eligibility

For standard, unused items, contact us within 7 days of delivery. The item should be unused, unaltered and returned with its original packaging and accessories. Approval may be required before a return is sent.

Non-returnable situations

Custom specifications, personalized work, used or knocked-in bats, customer-caused damage and ordinary natural willow variation may not qualify for change-of-mind returns unless required by law.

Damage in transit

Keep the packaging and send clear photos or video of the package and product promptly so the issue can be assessed.

Refunds

Approved refunds are returned through an appropriate payment method after inspection. Banking or payment-provider processing times can vary.
