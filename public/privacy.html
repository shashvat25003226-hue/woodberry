Privacy Policy

Effective: 19 October 2026

Woodberry Sports uses personal information only to operate the store, fulfil orders, provide support, secure accounts and improve the customer experience.

Information we collect

  • Account details such as name, email, phone number and password hash.
  • Delivery addresses and order information.
  • Reviews, support messages and notification preferences.
  • Technical information needed for security, sessions and basic service operation.

How we use information

We use information to authenticate users, process COD or online orders, deliver products, communicate order updates, prevent abuse and respond to customer requests.

Payments

When online payments are enabled, payment processing is handled by the configured payment provider. Woodberry Sports does not store full card details.

Data retention and deletion

Customers may delete their account from the account area. Some order records may need to be retained where required for accounting, fraud prevention, dispute handling or applicable law.

Sharing

We do not sell customer personal information. Information may be shared with service providers only when needed to run the store, process payments, host the service, deliver orders or comply with lawful requests.

Contact

Questions or privacy requests: notout@woodberrycricket.com.

This policy is an operational website policy and should be reviewed against the business's actual practices and applicable local law before large-scale commercial launch.
