GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked.

How much does a Woodberry bat cost?

Each standard Woodberry bat is priced at ₹10,000. Custom options and destination-dependent costs, if any, are quoted during the WhatsApp consultation.

Do you deliver internationally?

Yes, we accept worldwide delivery enquiries. Confirm your destination, address and delivery terms with our team before placing an order.

Can I order a custom bat?

Yes. Use the Custom Bat form to discuss weight, balance, profile, handle and other specifications directly with Woodberry Sports.

How do I place an order?

Select the Signature bat and choose Order on WhatsApp. Our team will confirm stock, price, delivery and payment details before accepting the order.

Is cash on delivery available?

Payment options vary by destination. Ask our team whether COD is available for your delivery address.

What about returns and damaged orders?

Contact Woodberry promptly with your order details and supporting photographs. Return eligibility and any shipping costs must be confirmed in the order terms.
