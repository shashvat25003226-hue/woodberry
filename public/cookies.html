Cookie Policy

Woodberry Sports uses a small number of cookies and browser-storage values to make the store work.

Essential cookies

The site uses an HTTP-only session cookie for signed-in customer and administrator sessions. This is required for authentication and security.

Browser storage

The browser may store cart contents, theme preference and cookie preference locally on your device so they remain available between visits.

Payments and third parties

If online payment is enabled, the payment provider may set or read its own cookies or storage when its checkout interface is opened. External social links and Google Fonts may also involve requests to third-party services.

Choice

The cookie banner lets visitors choose essential-only or accept the current site preferences. The store does not require advertising cookies for its core shopping functions.
