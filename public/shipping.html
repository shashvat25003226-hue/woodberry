Orders

Shipping Policy

Woodberry Sports currently advertises free delivery for the listed bat collection unless a checkout or support message states otherwise.

Address verification

Indian PIN codes can be used to suggest city, district and state information. Customers remain responsible for checking the complete delivery address before placing an order.

Dispatch and tracking

Orders are prepared after stock and payment/COD details are confirmed. Tracking information may be provided through the account notification area, email, phone or WhatsApp when available.

Delivery timing

Delivery estimates depend on destination, courier availability, weather, customs and other circumstances. Custom-prepared bats may take longer than ready-stock bats.

International orders

International recipients are responsible for any import duty, tax, customs fee or clearance requirement imposed by the destination country unless Woodberry Sports explicitly states otherwise in writing.

Delivery problems

Contact notout@woodberrycricket.com or WhatsApp promptly if tracking shows an issue or a parcel appears damaged.
